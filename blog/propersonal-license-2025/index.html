UModeler X Pro Personal - Unity 3D Modeling for Indie Creators

August 5, 2025 · 3 min read
Sora Chae
Marketing Manager

UModeler X Pro Personal License Release!

We’re thrilled to introduce the UModeler X Pro Personal License – launching August 5!
The UModeler X Pro Personal License officially launched on August 5! Whether you're a solo developer, student, or creative hobbyist, you can now access all the essential 3D editing features of UModeler X for just USD9/month. Upgrade your Unity workflow at an affordable price with the power of UModeler X.

💡 What is UModeler X Pro Personal License?​

For months, UModeler X has been available under our Pro and Enterprise tiers – supporting professional teams with a full suite of modeling, rigging, and painting tools inside Unity.

But we’ve heard you – many creators want access to the same power and workflow at a more accessible price.

Key Features of the Pro Personal Plan​

So here it is: UModeler X Pro Personal License.

  • ✅ Full access to 3D editing tools
  • ✅ Ongoing feature updates
  • ✅ Early access to new tools
  • ✅ PFL (Perpetual Fallback License) included
  • ✅ Usage limited only by scale – not by creativity
💬 Usage Eligibility

The Pro Personal License is only available to individuals or teams whose total revenue or funding was under $200,000 USD in the past 12 months.
If your organization exceeds this threshold, we recommend the Pro or Enterprise plans instead.

🎯 Who Is It For?​

  • Pro Personal – Solo creators, students, or indie developers with limited budgets
  • Pro – Indie dev teams or studios looking for more content
  • Enterprise – Full teams, universities, or production studios with complex needs

👉 Remember: Pro Personal License is only available to users or teams with < $200K revenue/funding in the past 12 months.

🎁 Special Upgrade Offers for Existing UModeler Users​

We’re offering exclusive upgrade benefits for our loyal users:

🆓 UModeler X Plus Users (from Unity Asset Store)​

  • 1-year Personal License — FREE
  • This effectively grants you a Perpetual Fallback License (PFL) right away — no subscription required.

💸 UModeler (Legacy) Users​

  • 30% OFF your first-year Personal subscription
  • Maintain your subscription for one year to receive a PFL (permanent license) at the end.

📩 How to Upgrade to UModeler X Pro Personal​

Go to our Pricing Page and choose either the Pro Personal or Pro plan.
A field will appear where you can enter your Unity Asset Store invoice number to apply your discount.
